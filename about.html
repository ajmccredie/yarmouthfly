About Us

Our Vision & Venue

Traditionally, flying trapeze is a troupe act rarely performed outside of circus big tops. While it has grown in popularity as a recreational hobby over the last few decades, most UK venues only offer seasonal training on outdoor structures, with limited access to professional industry connections.

Under the close eye of director Mat Herman, Flighthouse aims to bridge that gap. With a heavy focus on strong foundations and mastering key skills, we provide a year-round, indoor facility that nurtures local beginners while simultaneously offering a direct pipeline for serious flyers to enter the global entertainment industry.


Why Train at Flighthouse?

  • Year-round indoor rigging — no rain, no wind, and fully heated training conditions on a performance rig designed for professionals.
  • The ultimate perks — proudly the only flying trapeze school in the country featuring plumbed-in toilets and our own on-site speakeasy bar.
  • Live performance opportunities — working in close collaboration with the Jay brothers, we host regular performance nights and public circus shows, giving professional pathway students invaluable experience performing in front of live audiences.

Accommodation & Funding

To support our community, we are able to offer on-site accommodation for select intensive courses, in collaboration with the Jay family. We also offer subsidised funding opportunities for specific groups and individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds in the interest of making flying trapeze accessible to everyone, regardless of financial background. More on this as we develop.

The Team

Flighthouse is the brainchild of Mat collective years on the road. It represents years of dreaming, industry frustration, and a stubborn refusal to give up—finally manifesting into a world-class training facility that remarkably features both a roof and heating.

Our coaching roster is dynamic and constantly evolving. We pull in active industry professionals who rotate through the space between global show obligations, ensuring you always learn from current, working circus artists.

Mat

Mat Herman | Founder & Head Chef

Mat brings over 15 years of professional touring experience as a solo freelancer, troupe manager, and circus rigger. Using his extensive engineering knowledge, he initially designed a complex, in-house system allowing him to work the platform, catch, and pull safety lines all at the exact same time. However, it turns out doing all three simultaneously is deceptively difficult. Accepting reality, he gave Danny a ring to come on board as his coaching sous-chef.

When he isn't performing or hanging from circus rigs, Mat is a skilled metalworker and welder. Over the last few years, he has built a strong reputation as one of the UK’s top up-and-coming prop makers, specialising specifically in flying rigging. Mat is keen to merge his dual passions—fabrication and performance—to train, support, and help create the next generation of professional British circus troupes.
