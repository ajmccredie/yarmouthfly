Flying Trapeze and Circus Training

Welcome to Flighthouse

The UK’s newest year-round, indoor flying trapeze and circus arts venue.

Located inside the historic Empire Theatre, Great Yarmouth, Flighthouse is a world-class training facility born from a collaboration between Steal This Circus and Hippodrome Circus.

What We Offer

  • Full-sized Flying Trapeze training on a structure designed for performance, casting cradle, and top catch.
  • All skill levels welcome — introductory courses for absolute beginners, intensive weekend workshops for recreational flyers, and open fly sessions for industry professionals.
  • The Industry Pipeline — a dedicated pathway designed to train new performing troupes and connect fresh talent to global circus productions.
