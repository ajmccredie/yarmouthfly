Welcome to Flighthouse

The UK’s newest year-round, indoor flying trapeze and circus arts venue.

Located inside the historic Empire Theatre in Great Yarmouth, Flighthouse is a collaboration between Steal This Circus and Hippodrome Circus, offering a professional-grade training facility for flying trapeze and swinging disciplines.

What We Offer

  • Full-sized flying trapeze with professional dimensions and a cradle top catch.
  • Indoor training facilities with space for cloud swing, swinging trapeze, and cradle casting.
  • Introductory courses, intensive workshops, and open training sessions.
  • The Industry Pipeline — a dedicated development programme designed to train new artists and connect emerging talent with professional productions worldwide.

Train, Explore, Progress

Flighthouse is built around strong technical foundations, safe progression, and meaningful pathways into professional circus work.

Our training structure is designed to make new flyers feel welcome while providing experienced students and aspiring professionals with the facilities, coaching, and industry connections needed to take the next step in their development.
