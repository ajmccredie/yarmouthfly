Flying Trapeze and Circus Training

Welcome to Flighthouse


The newest UK venue for flying trapeze and circus arts, located in the Empire Theatre, Great Yarmouth, Norfolk.


A collaboration between Steal This Circus, and Hippodrome Circus; this venue will offer full time professional training and development for grand volant flying trapeze, and casting cradle and top catch, in a year round, indoor facility.


Our workshops and classes are tailored for multiple levels, from introductory courses for beginners, intensive workshops for recreational flyers, and open fly sessions for industry professionals.


Drawing on our collective knowledge of the professional circus industry, we hope this project will create a pathway for new flyers and performing troupes, and that our school may act as an agency in connecting new talent to existing shows across the world.


Installation has already begun, and we plan to open our doors to the general public this summer August 2026.


Stay tuned for further updates.
