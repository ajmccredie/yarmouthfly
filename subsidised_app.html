Subsidised Under-25s Flying Programme

Calling Flyers of Norfolk & Great Yarmouth!

Thanks to our CIC status, we are proud to offer a heavily subsidised, rolling four-week flying trapeze programme for young people aged under 25.

Our first programme launches on Tuesday 29 September 2026, with a new four-week block planned to run each month throughout the winter.

4 Weeks

One Group

Each programme is for a fixed group of up to 10 young people who train together throughout the entire four-week block.

Twice Weekly

8 Sessions

The group trains every Tuesday and Thursday, 4:00 pm–6:00 pm, for four consecutive weeks.

Subsidised

£100 Total

The complete eight-session programme costs just £100 — equivalent to £12.50 per session.

Your Place Is For The Whole Programme

This is a progressive group programme rather than a collection of individual drop-in classes.

If you are offered a place, you are committing to attend all eight sessions: every Tuesday and Thursday for the full four weeks.

The same group will train together throughout the block. This allows everyone to progress together, build confidence, support one another and develop as a strong community of new flyers.

And There’s A Show!

At the end of our first four-week block, participants will be invited to perform in FlightHouse's first-ever community show on Friday 30 October 2026.

Who Are The Subsidised Places For?

We want these funded opportunities to reach young people who may otherwise find regular flying trapeze training difficult to access.

Priority will therefore be given to applicants experiencing financial, social or other barriers to participation. The application form below helps us understand your circumstances and allocate the limited subsidised places fairly.

Applicants under 16 should complete the application with input from a parent or guardian, while answering the questions from the applicant's point of view.

Please note: Completing the form is an application for a subsidised place and does not automatically guarantee a place on the programme.
