Application Questionnaire for Subsidised Flying Opportunities

This application is used for the under-25s youth development programme, professional development workshop programme, and individual training bursaries.

Applicants under 16 must have parental or guardian input. The application should still be completed from the applicant's point of view.

Fields marked * are required.

1. Applicant details

Please complete this if the applicant is under 16.

2. Regular sports or training costs

Do you currently pay for any regular sports activities, training or memberships?

This might include gym membership, sports classes, circus training, dance classes, coaching or similar activities.

3. Existing bursaries or funding

Do you currently receive any bursaries, grants, discounts or other funding towards training opportunities?

For example: subsidised circus training, personal grants, funded training, or reduced class rates due to financial circumstances.

4. Employment or occupation status

What is your current employment or occupation status?

If you are over 18, please answer for yourself. If you are under 18, please answer based on the highest earner in your household.
